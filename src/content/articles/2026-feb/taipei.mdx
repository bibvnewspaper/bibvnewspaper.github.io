## Taipei 101 

By Vinnie Hong (8Ti)

On January 25, 2026, Alex Honnold, an already renowned rock climber from California, did a free solo ascent on Taiwan’s Taipei 101. With an accomplished time of 1 hour and 31 minutes climbing the near 2000-foot tower, it was beyond surreal to see Honnold rise into the sky. Honnold’s feat was not only a challenge proposed by Netflix’s Skyscraper Live, but also a shock to everyone by placing him as the first person to complete the highest urban free solo ever: to put into perspective, that’s two records broken in just one climb!

The challenges of climbing a 101-story skyscraper demand resilience, courage, and most importantly, good equipment. Alex Honnold made the task possible without any equipment; in fact, he did it without ropes or a harness, which is what climbers call “free-soloing”. He repeatedly tackled the building’s distinctive stepped-pogada-style sections, or what Honnold called “bamboo boxes,” using the balcony rests  that follow after each of the 8 mid sections.
