By Megna Prakash (8Ti)

 From February 9th to 13th, we celebrated Kindness week with a special spirit week dedicated to spreading positivity in the BIBV community. Each day of our spirit week featured a different theme. Our spirit week began with “Better Together” Day, where students dressed similar or same to their friends. Tuesday was “Celebrate Differences Day”, where students wore intentionally mismatched clothes to celebrate everyone’s individuality and uniqueness. Students also celebrated “Color the  World with Kindness Day”, wearing tie-dye and bright colors. On Thursday, students wore comfy pajamas to school to show the importance of caring for themselves and others. Each day included a kindness activity that gave everyone the chance to practice kindness in a fun and hands-on way. Students had the opportunity to write and exchange kind letters to other students during AE, add to a kindness mural, enjoy music at recess, and create origami flowers. Kindness week coincided with the Lunar New Year Celebrations, making it a lively and exciting week on campus. Our Kindness  week is connected with the global random acts of kindness day on Feb 17th, which is a significant day celebrated worldwide, that encourages people to perform small gestures of kindness in their daily life. The Random Acts of Kindness Day was founded in 1995 by the Random Acts of Kindness Foundation.
