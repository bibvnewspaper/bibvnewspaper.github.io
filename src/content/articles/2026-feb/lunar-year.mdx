## Lunar New Year

By Sam Liu (8Ti)

The Lunar New Year celebration at BASIS Independent Bellevue was really fun and full of different activities for students to try. One of the most popular stations was the candy‑drawing game, where everyone got to draw pictures using candy and also eat them. There was also a calligraphy table where students could practice writing traditional characters and learn what they meant. Around the room, there were other booths that showed different parts of Lunar New Year traditions, like decorations, picture taking, and riddles. The whole event felt lively and exciting, with lots of people walking around and trying new things. Happy Lunar New Year!
