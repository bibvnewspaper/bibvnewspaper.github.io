## Ingredients

Homemade Salted Caramel:
½ cup sugar
3 tbsp room temp unsalted butter (it needs to be room temp! THIS IS IMPORTANT)
¼ cup warm heavy cream (it needs to be warm! THIS IS IMPORTANT)
½ tsp vanilla extract
½ tsp salt

Chocolate Cupcakes
¾ cup all-purpose flour
⅓ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
1 tsp espresso powder
½ tsp salt
¾ tsp baking soda
½ cup room temp unsalted butter
1 cup sugar
2 large room temp eggs
1 ½ tsp vanilla extract
½ cup room temp sour cream
⅔ cup strong, hot coffee

Salted Caramel Buttercream
½ cup chilled salted caramel
1 cup room temp unsalted butter
1 ½ - 2 cups powdered sugar
2 tbsp heavy cream


## Instructions

Making the salted caramel:

Make sure that the heavy cream is warm (not hot, not room temp) and the unsalted butter is at room temperature
In a medium saucepan, add the sugar in an even layer and melt over medium heat; use a rubber spatula and nudge at the edges to make sure none of the sugar on the side burns or sticks to the pan
As clumps start to form, gently stir
Once melted, the sugar should be golden (if it’s dark brown, skip this step); stop mixing it immediately and wait ~30 sec for it to caramelize and darken
Immediately turn off heat and whisk in all the butter all at once for ~30 sec
Quickly add the heavy cream and continue to whisk until smooth and fluid; then add vanilla extract and salt (if it starts to clump up again, put it on medium heat and stir until the clumps melt)
Transfer to a heat-safe container and place in the fridge to chill 


Making the cupcakes:

Preheat oven to 350F & place 12 cupcake liners in a cupcake tin
In a medium bowl, sift and whisk together flour, cocoa powder, espresso powder, salt, and baking soda; then set aside
Using an electric mixer of some sort (whether it’s handheld or a stand), mix together butter and sugar until light and fluffy (should take ~3 minutes)
Mix in the eggs one at a time
Mix in vanilla extract and sour cream
Add half the dry ingredients (don’t forget to periodically scrape down the bowl), then the coffee, then the rest of the flour mix
Evenly distribute batter in the cupcake tin, then bake for ~15 min, or until a toothpick comes out clean/ with a few moist, not wet, crumbs
Let them rest and cool down before frosting the cupcakes

Making the buttercream:

Using an electric mixer, whip caramel on high until it lightens and forms soft peaks (~2 min)
Add the room temp butter (it’s important that the butter is room temperature, or else the buttercream won’t be smooth and it will be very difficult to frost) and whip again until smooth
Mix in heavy cream (after this, frosting should be thick and fluffy)
Take about 1 cup of frosting and melt it in the microwave
Add the melted buttercream back into the bowl and whip until smooth
Frost the cupcakes when they’re completely cool 🙂
