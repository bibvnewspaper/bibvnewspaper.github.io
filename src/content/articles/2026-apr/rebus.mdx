Rebus made by Vinnie Hong 8Ti
Explanation written by Stephanie Song 8Pt

First, we see a woman and we can replace that with a "miss" then combined with "tur" and "D" makes "Mister D" or "Mr. D" 
Then combine it with the words given to us right after to get "Mr. D is the..." Now we see a woman grimacing because of the noise and we can replace that with "loudest" to get "Mr. D is the loudest..."
Then we see a teacher and and a green power button signaling on. We can add that to our message to get "Mr. D is the loudest teacher on..." Then, we see a camp with tents and the US flag. We combine camp and US to get "campus". Add that to our message along with the and that is given. "Mr. D is the loudest teacher on campus and"
This is where it gets harder. There is a picture of a yellow guy moving his hand side to side signaling so-so. Now take one "so" and add it to our answer along with the "is" that is given. "Mr. D is the loudest teacher on campus and so is..."
Now we see another woman again but without a tur next to it so it must be a "Ms" to get "Mr. D is the loudest teacher on campus and so is Ms."
Then we see a no mountain sign along with a picture of a mountain labeled cerro nosoeste. Cerro Noroeste is often referred to as mount abel, and if we take off the mount from that, we get "abel"
Combined with the e that is given, we get "Abele"

Now we have our answer: Mr. D is the loudest teacher on campus and so is Ms. Abele!
