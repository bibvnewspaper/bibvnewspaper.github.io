## Mother's Day

By Sam Liu (8Ti)

 Mother’s Day is a holiday in May that is celebrated every year on the second Sunday in May and it is a day when people take time to show love and appreciation for their mothers as well as other mother figures in their lives,  many people celebrate by giving flowers, writing cards, making breakfast, going out to eat, and spending time together at home, and even something like a phone call or a hug can mean a lot, because the day is really about noticing all the things mothers do every day that often go unseen like caring, supporting, and helping their families, and while gifts are good, the most important part is saying thank you and showing love in a simple honest way, and for many people it is also a reminder appreciate the people who are always there for them even in small everyday moments.
