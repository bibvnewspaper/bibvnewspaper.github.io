## Guess the Drink with Ms. Abele

Suri: First question, this is something from Mr. D, what are your thoughts on the name ‘Jabele’?

Ms. Abele: *laughs* My thoughts are that Ms. Bryant called me Jabele first. I mean, no, I love it. It’s become a part of my personality, apparently.

Suri: I never heard anyone call you that.

Ms. Abele: The kids have started calling me Jabele. They really tried. And I’m like, that’s not my name. But yeah, it’s pretty cool.

Suri: But, like, the people who do it the most. Like, to annoy you.

Ms. Abele: Just to annoy me? I don’t know, I think seventh grade pretty regularly calls me Jabele. Or tries. 

Suri: Okay, uh, take a sip of any one you want.

Ms. Abele: I’m scared.

Ms. Abele: *takes a sip, contemplates* That’s a Doctor Pepper. Diet.

Audience: *in awe of her amazing guessing skills*

Ms. Abele: That is aspartame, that is not DP Zero.

Audience: *clamors, voicing their shock*

Ms. Abele: Guys, don’t mess with me, don’t even play with me. I drink a Doctor Pepper almost daily. Doctor Pepper a day keeps the doctor away.

Suri: What is your favorite mammal?

Ms. Abele: My favorite animal…a moose. I love a moose. Those things are giant, they’re terrifying, and they are so majestic to see in the wild.

Suri: Have you seen one in the wild?

Ms. Abele: Yes, it’s so cool. Like, you cannot believe how big they are when you actually see them.

Ms Abele: *takes a sip of a not energy drink* But this is absolutely an energy drink. A hundred percent. If I were to get crazy extra specific, I’d say a cotton candy Bang.

Suri: I’ll tell you that you’re wrong.

Ms. Abele: But it does kind of taste like a cotton candy Bang. I don’t know. Should I try a different one?

Suri: If you answer another question.

Ms. Abele: Okay, ask away.

Suri: What is your order at the Thai place you go to at Crossroads?

Ms. Abele: *laughs* I get a pad thai with tofu, two stars spicy, with noodles only, no salad, because then they double it, and then they give you too many noodles and I have leftovers, and I get two thai teas, because I drink the first one too fast, and then I get upset when it’s gone. They called me out the last time. 

Ms. Abele: *takes a drink of a White Monster* it’s like an orange something. Is this one an energy drink?

Audience: Not quite. Like, more not than quite.

Ms. Abele: Okay, is it like an orange crush? Like an orange soda?

Suri: Strive away from orange.

Ms. Abele: What do you mean, strive away from orange? My taste buds don’t lie.

Audience: But what if they do?

Ms. Abele: Then call up the doctor, Ms. Ruby’s out today, I don’t know what we’re going to do. *...* Is it a white monster?

Suri: Yes!

Suri: Would you rather watch a Mr. Norcross-voice-acted Shrek movie or hear Mr. Norcross speak in a Mike Meyer Shrek accent for the whole day?

Ms. Abele: I already do hear Shrek when he speaks. So, absolutely the former. And actually, him voice overing every character. That would be so funny. 

Ms. Abele: *takes a sip of indicated drink, adopts perplexed expression* It doesn’t really taste like anything. 

Suri: Should we just show you? You’re not going to guess it.

*reveals the ‘Keep it Green’ Lemon-Lime Elixir (which is Shrek-themed)*

Ms. Abele: *laughs* That’s SO funny. We should leave this on his desk.
