## Student Appreciation Week!

By Molly Miao (8Ti)

This year’s student appreciation week lasted from May 18 to May 22, and featured a special event for each day of the week. On Monday, students received  boba. Board games were played on Tuesday. Students who showed up could play a variety of card and board games with their friends. On Wednesday, ice cream with a side of cookie dough was handed out from an ice cream truck on school campus. The cookie dough could be either chocolate chip, sugar cookie, or Oreo flavoured. Puzzles were presented during AE on Thursday, and students could receive participation prizes from working on them. On Friday, teachers played games of kickball against students.
