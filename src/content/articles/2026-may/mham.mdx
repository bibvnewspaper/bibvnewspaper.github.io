## Mental Health Awareness Month

By: Vinnie Hong (8Ti)

 Mental Health Awareness Month arrives every May, reminding us to pause and check in with ourselves. It’s not really a lecture; imagine it as that one friend who shows up with snacks, sits directly in front of you, and says, “Alright, so how are you really doing right now?”

This month is about noticing the smaller things in our view, the small things that we often brush aside. The most common include small streaks of stress that slip in between classes, worries that only accumulate in the night, and moments when everything feels “fine” at first, but it’s also not fine. Mental Health Awareness Month encourages us to identify those feelings instead of hoping they’ll go away at some point. 

It’s also a reminder that if you're feeling a certain strange way, you’re not alone. Seriously, you’re not alone. There are others that face the same difficulties you’re facing, wishing they’ll disappear with one snap of a finger. However, it’s greatly encouraged to find a way, any way, to reduce that stress, worriness, or anything else. Gathering up courage and talking about it with a trusted adult always helps, along with taking small breaks when you feel like you need it. Over time, these small acts stack up, and they matter. This is the real power of awareness: it turns quite struggles into a shared understanding.
