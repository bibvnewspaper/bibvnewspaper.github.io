## The Dreaded End of Year Exams

By Clara Jeon (8Ti)

With the dreaded comps approaching day by day (starting on June 9th), all of the middle schoolers and some of the fifth graders are hard at work, studying for their final comprehensive exams. Study guides, practice worksheets, mock tests, they’re all piling up in large mountains that will likely stand taller than them. Although some of the students are quite tall. Teachers and staff have been wishing everyone good luck on their studies and encouraging them to take breaks whilst working, to ensure they have ample time to relax and have some time to themselves before the actual exams. In addition to these tests, they will enjoy a field trip afterwards at High Trek Adventures, so middle schoolers, look forward to this relief in the final stretch of the trimester!

Comp schedules will be the same as previous years, with English, Physics, Chemistry on the first day, then History and Math before World Language and Biology to finish it all off.

The newspaper team wishes you all the best in your studies!

Keep up the good work!
