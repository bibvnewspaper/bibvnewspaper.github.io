## Grades 3-4 Comics!

By Danika Pierce (10)

The BASIS Independent Bellevue Elementary school students of Mr. Brad’s class have exercised their imaginations with the creation of humorous comics. Students put their creativity to paper with the construction of the panels. With each child, comes a different perspective. For instance, the setting of the comic, “The Picnic” , by Meng, 4GE, is the Eiffel Tower, while the comic, “Area 51 be like” by Aiden Yin takes the reader to, as one would expect by the title, area 51!

Mr. Brad’s childhood dreams of being a cartoonist inspired him to teach his elementary art students, of third and fourth grade, how to draw comics. As explained by Mr. Brad, each student could pick two animals from a list with one blank option, which gave the students the option to choose from the full animal kingdom. Additionally, the students selected two contrasting personality traits for their creatures. 
This helped the students to create conflict between the characters and reflected numerous professional comics that use this tactic like the Elephant and Piggie book series. They were given six panels to exercise their creation upon and to learn artistic skills, such as perspective and attention to details. Mr. Brad also encouraged students to not second guess themselves, as he said art to be, "uniquely yours.” 

On a more humorous note, Mr. Brad observed and enjoyed students' "kooky ideas”. For instance, a student, when presented with the animal choices, decided upon a mosquito rather than what would be widely considered as a “cute” animal. Mr. Brad said, in reference to this project, “the personality of the artist is what speaks to you,” and that he simply wants his students to be, "unapologetically themselves.”
